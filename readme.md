# AKAN NAME GENERATOR

A small web app that works out the day of the week you were born and gives you the traditional Akan name that matches your birth day and gender.

## Author
MARYANNE W. KINYUA

## How it works

1. Enter your birth day, month, year, and gender.
2. The app checks your date is valid.
3. It calculates the day of the week using a date formula.
4. It shows you the matching ghanian traditional akan name.

## Files

- `index.html` — the page and form
- `styles.css` — the look of the page
- `script.js` — the logic (validation, calculation, and display)


## Built with

HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
